I am a computational and translational biologist with 10+ years of experience
integrating single-cell, spatial, bulk multi-omics, and proteomics to generate
mechanistic insight, biomarker hypotheses, and therapeutic strategies in
oncology and neurological disease. My work spans a hybrid of experimental and
computational biology, contributing end-to-end from study design, assay design,
and _in vivo_ modeling through multi-omics analysis, artifact detection, and
interpretation.

My current research asks how **biological sex** shapes immune function, therapeutic
response, and neuroimmune disease biology at the intersection of cancer
immunology, sex biased immune responses, neuroinflammation, and computational biology. Recent first- and
co-first-author studies in _Cancer Cell_ and _Nature Communications_ span tumor
immunology, sex-biased immune evasion (the male-biased YAP1–CD276/B7-H3 axis in
medulloblastoma), clinical-trial molecular discovery in colorectal cancer, and
pan-cancer myeloid biology.

Alongside the biology, I build **open-source software** that makes rigorous
single-cell and spatial analysis reproducible and teachable — including
[scSidekick](/projects/scsidekick/), [RNAseqWizard](/projects/rnaseqwizard/),
and [MatchLR](/projects/matchlr/). See the [publications](/publications/) and
[projects](/projects/) pages for details, or read the [CV](/cv/).
