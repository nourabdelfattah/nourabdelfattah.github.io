**scSidekick** is an open-source R package that takes users from raw count
matrices through quality control, integration, clustering, cell-type assignment,
differential expression, pathway analysis, and cell–cell communication — while
enforcing documentation at every step and producing publication-ready figures,
methods text, legends, and PowerPoint exports.

It implements multi-group comparisons, CellChat, inferCNV, cNMF, pseudotime,
pseudobulk analysis, ssGSEA, and survival-linked clinical correlation workflows.
